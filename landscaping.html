Professional Landscaping Services

Keeping It Green has partnered with Second Nature Landscape Services, LLC to provide comprehensive landscaping solutions for your property.

Whether you need regular maintenance to keep your property looking its best or a complete landscape transformation, our partnership allows us to offer a full range of landscaping services to meet your needs.

Services Include

Weekly & Bi-Weekly Mowing

Professional lawn mowing services on a schedule that works for you. We keep your grass at the optimal height for health and appearance.

Mulching of Flower Beds

Fresh mulch application to protect plants, retain moisture, suppress weeds, and give your beds a polished, finished look.

Bush Trimming

Keep your shrubs and hedges perfectly shaped with regular trimming services that promote healthy growth.

Tree Pruning

Professional pruning to maintain tree health, improve structure, and enhance the beauty of your landscape.

Landscape Design & Installation

Complete landscape transformation services from initial design concepts to professional installation.
