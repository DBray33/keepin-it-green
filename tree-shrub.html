Tree & Shrub Care Services

Your trees and shrubs are valuable investments that enhance the beauty and value of your property. At Keeping It Green, we provide comprehensive care to keep them healthy, vibrant, and thriving year-round.

Our certified professionals understand the unique challenges that trees and shrubs face in Berks County. From pest infestations to nutrient deficiencies, we diagnose issues early and apply targeted treatments to protect your landscape investments.

Our Services Include

Health Evaluations

Comprehensive assessments to identify diseases, pest damage, and nutrient deficiencies before they become major problems.

Fertilization Programs

Custom nutrient programs designed to promote strong root development and vibrant foliage throughout the growing season.

Disease & Pest Control

Targeted treatments to eliminate common tree and shrub pests including aphids, scale, mites, and fungal diseases.

Seasonal Treatments

Year-round care programs that address the specific needs of your trees and shrubs during each season.

Why Tree & Shrub Care Matters

Trees and shrubs face constant threats from environmental stress, pests, and diseases. Without proper care, these problems can spread quickly, causing permanent damage or even killing your plants.

Our proactive approach focuses on prevention rather than reaction. By maintaining the health of your trees and shrubs throughout the year, we help them develop stronger natural defenses against common problems.

As licensed pesticide applicators certified by the state of Pennsylvania, we use products and techniques that are effective yet safe for your family, pets, and the environment.
